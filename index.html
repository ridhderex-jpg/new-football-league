How the league works

Teams play their division rivals twice a year, once at home and once away. Playing the same opponents that often builds the rivalries.

4 divisionsEast, North, South and West
2 gamesagainst each division rival, home and away
1 bye weekfor every division
50 playerson the roster, 44 active on game day

League format is a working draft and may change before the first season.
